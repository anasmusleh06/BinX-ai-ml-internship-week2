# Day 2 - Probability & Distributions

**Overview:**

This notebook covers the math foundations. It focuses on core probability concepts and how they connect to machine learning: simulating random processes, working with the normal distribution and computing conditional probability both by hand and through simulation.

**Contents:**
- Step 1 — Coin Flip Simulation: Simulated 10,000 fair coin flips with NumPy and confirmed the proportion of heads converges to 0.5 (Law of Large Numbers).
- Step 2 — Normal Distribution Sampling: Generated 10,000 samples from a normal distribution using np.random.normal, plotted a histogram and confirmed the bell-shaped curve visually and numerically.
- Step 3 — Conditional Probability: Worked out P(King | Face card) from a standard deck by hand (1/3), then verified the result with a Monte Carlo simulation over 100,000 trials.
- Step 4 — Summary: Documented what each experiment demonstrates and how it ties back to Day 2’s theory (probability rules, distributions and the foundation for Bayes’ theorem).

**Tools Used:**
NumPy • Matplotlib • Jupyter Notebook
